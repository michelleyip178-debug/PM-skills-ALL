## Description

Objective: 

To define and document a comprehensive Quality Assurance (QA) strategy for the *PSD OTEP - MVP*. This strategy aims to support rapid development within a 3-month timeline while managing the inherent risks. 

Key Deliverable:

# QA Strategy Document: A formal document (Confluence) outlining the testing approach including the scope, environment map and testing metrics.

Acceptance criteria:

* QA Strategy document is reviewed and approved by EM, TL and Product Owner.
* Testing layers (Unit/Integration/Contract/E2E) are clearly defined with assigned responsibilities.
* The strategy is integrated into the 12-week MVP roadmap.
