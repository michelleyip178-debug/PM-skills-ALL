h3. What it verifies

An officer with a Job Family but *no Job Function*, where Job-Family-only returns *insufficient* candidates, should have the search *expand to Agency*. If the agency returns more than required, the excess is *randomised* down to the required count.

h3. Test Data / Setup

*Job Family used: Financial Services* (Enterprise Singapore) , Grade 10

h3. Test Steps

# Log in as *Richard_RAMOS_FROM.TP@cscollege.gov.sg*.

# Note the vertical roles and their family; *refresh* the page a few times and compare.

h3. Expected Result

Search expands to Agency when Job Family alone is insufficient; excess candidates are randomised down to the required count (so the roles vary on refresh).
