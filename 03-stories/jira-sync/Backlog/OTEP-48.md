**Michelle Yip** (2026-03-05)
From Meeting with Orion - 4 Mar 2026 How STIPs and gigs relate to the ATS vs OTG Suitability for the new ATS The team questioned whether STIPs and gigs roles are suitable to be posted on the ATS at all, or whether they should continue to be handled in OTG/OTEP instead. They noted that the ATS is primarily designed for more structured recruitment processes (e.g., those that clearly include stages like shortlisting, interviews, assessments). For gigs, there was concern about low application volume and whether it's "worth it" to customize ATS flows for them. For STIPs, they highlighted that these may not always involve interviews or the full structured funnel, so they might not align well with the ATS' intended use. Process & stages considerations They discussed that if ATS's core objective is to improve the funnel for structured processes that require interviews and similar stages, then: Some states/roles (like STIPs and certain gigs) might not fit that model and may be better left outside ATS. The team asked for clarity on which stages or role types are "in scope" for ATS, and explicitly requested that if STIPs/gigs are not suitable, this should be made clear, so OTG/OTEP can plan to support them separately. Desire to avoid multiple platforms for hiring managers They expressed a strong wish to avoid forcing hiring managers to juggle two systems: One for "normal" roles via ATS Another for STIPs/gigs via OTG/OTEP. However, they acknowledged that if ATS cannot flexibly support these atypical flows, then some separation might be unavoidable. Open questions / next steps They agreed they need more understanding of gigs specifically before deciding if it's "worth" customizing ATS flows for them. The conversation positioned STIPs and gigs as edge cases that may stay with OTG/OTEP, while ATS focuses on mainstream recruitment pipelines. In summary, in the Orion meeting they didn't finalize a decision, but raised concerns that STIPs and gigs might not be a good fit for the new ATS and may need to remain under OTG/OTEP, unless the ATS can be flexibly customized to those lighter, less-structured workflows.
