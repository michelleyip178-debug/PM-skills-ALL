# OTEP-394: Support competency tagging for Positions

**Type:** Story
**Status:** Backlog
**Assignee:** N/A
**Story Points:** N/A

---

## Description

During recent review, it was identified that competencies can also be tagged to  Positions , in addition to existing entities (job IDs). This capability was  not considered within the MVP scope , where competency tagging is currently limited to manually tagged opportunities. As part of future iterations, we should assess and implement support for tagging competencies at the  Position level , to ensure consistency across job-based features and enable more accurate competency mapping (e.g. for gap analysis, recommendations, and career insights).
