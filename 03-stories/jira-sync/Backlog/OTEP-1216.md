# OTEP-1216: Posthog - Feedback rating

**Status:** Backlog
**Assignee:** N/A
**Story Points:** N/A

## Description

Objective Instrument the Career Compass satisfaction feedback component in PostHog so that we can measure officer satisfaction as a key MVP success metric, understand where ratings are submitted from, and measure how many officers choose to provide additional feedback through FormSG. Event 1:  satisfaction_rating Triggered when an officer selects a satisfaction rating and successfully clicks  Submit . Property Description rating_value Rating submitted by the officer, from 1 to 6 source_page Page the officer was on when the satisfaction rating was submitted Acceptance Criteria Trigger  satisfaction_rating  only when the officer successfully submits their selected rating by clicking  Submit . Selecting or hovering over a star without clicking Submit must not trigger  satisfaction_rating . rating_value  must capture the final submitted value from  1 to 5 . source_page  must capture the page the officer was viewing at the point the rating was submitted. Only one  satisfaction_rating  event can be submitted by the same officer within the same session, in line with the feedback component behaviour.  Event 2:  survey_formsg_clicks Triggered when an officer clicks  “Tell us here.”  after successfully submitting their satisfaction rating. Property Description rating_value Satisfaction rating previously submitted by the officer in the same session source_page Page from which the satisfaction rating was originally submitted Acceptance Criteria Trigger  survey_formsg  when the officer clicks  “Tell us here.”  from the post-rating confirmation state. Displaying the  “Tell us here.”  CTA without the officer clicking it must not trigger  survey_formsg . rating_value  must capture the rating previously submitted by the officer within the same session. source_page  must retain the page from which the original satisfaction rating was submitted. The event should be captured before the officer is redirected to FormSG. survey_formsg  represents a click-through to the FormSG survey only and must not be interpreted as completion of the FormSG survey. Success Metrics The instrumentation should enable Product to measure: Overall satisfaction rating distribution Number and percentage of submitted ratings at each value from 1–6. Satisfaction by page Distribution of submitted ratings by  source_page . Rating participation Number of officers/sessions that submit a satisfaction rating. FormSG click-through rate Percentage of satisfaction-rating submissions that subsequently click  “Tell us here.” . FormSG click-through rate = unique sessions with  survey_formsg  / unique sessions with  satisfaction_rating  × 100 FormSG drop-off Percentage of officers who submit a satisfaction rating but do not subsequently click  “Tell us here.” . FormSG click-through by rating Compare the likelihood of clicking through to FormSG across different  rating_value  values to understand which satisfaction groups are more likely to provide additional qualitative feedback. Definition of Done satisfaction_rating  is implemented and fires only after a satisfaction rating is successfully submitted. survey_formsg  is implemented and fires only when  “Tell us here.”  is clicked. Both events contain the properties specified in this ticket. rating_value  is correctly captured as a value from 1–6. source_page  correctly identifies where the officer submitted their satisfaction rating. A maximum of one  satisfaction_rating  event can be submitted by the same officer within a session. rating_value  and  source_page  are correctly carried across to  survey_formsg . Events and their properties can be successfully validated in PostHog during testing. Product can use the captured data to analyse satisfaction-rating distribution, satisfaction by page, FormSG click-through, and FormSG drop-off.
