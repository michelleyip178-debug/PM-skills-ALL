## Description

User Story As an officer, I want to easily rate my experience on Career Compass so that I can quickly provide feedback on my satisfaction with the product. Background / Objective CSAT is a critical MVP success metric for Career Compass. Career Compass is not a transactional product and does not have a clear end-of-journey point. The feedback mechanism should therefore remain persistently discoverable throughout the officer's experience. The preferred placement is a persistent floating widget. Design References LifeSG Feedback Rating component. Please note that we have to customise the rating to a scale of 6 stars instead of 5     SMGS reference for post-submission feedback prompt:  https://smgs-uat.scholarships.gov.sg/ Acceptance Criteria IMPT: This ticket needs to be worked on together with      OTEP 1212 needs to be completed before OTEP 1215 Feedback Entry Point Officers can see a feedback rating component displaying the copy  "How's your experience today?" The feedback entry point should be persistently available throughout the Career Compass experience. The preferred placement is a persistent floating widget at the bottom corner of the page. The feedback component displays a  6-star rating scale . No rating is selected by default. Rating Selection and Submission Officers can select one of the six stars to indicate their satisfaction rating. Selecting a star does not immediately submit the rating. Officers must click the  Submit  button to submit their selected rating, following the existing interaction pattern of the LifeSG Feedback Rating component. A rating must be selected before it can be submitted. Once successfully submitted, the selected rating is recorded as the officer's satisfaction response for that session. An officer can submit a maximum of  one satisfaction rating per session . Once a rating has been successfully submitted, the officer cannot change or submit another rating within the same session. Post-Submission State After the rating is successfully submitted, the rating component is replaced by a confirmation state displaying: "Thank you! Have more feedback?" The confirmation state includes a button labelled  "Tell us here." Clicking  "Tell us here."  opens the designated FormSG feedback form. Completing the FormSG form is optional and is not required for the officer's satisfaction rating to be recorded. The satisfaction rating must be successfully recorded before the optional FormSG feedback CTA is displayed
