## Description

Acceptance criteria:  When i click on the tooltip link, it should open a CareerCompass themed page and show 5 opportunity types. Jobs (Internal / Secondments / External)  Job openings open to both Public Service officers and external candidates. STIPs (Short-Term Immersion Programmes)  Short-term attachments designed for learning and exposure in a different role, function, or agency. Gigs  Flexible, project-based opportunities you can take on alongside your current role to gain experience or contribute your skills. Careers@Gov Jobs  Public job openings open to both internal and external candidates, offering visibility into roles across the Public Service. SJRs (Structured Job Rotations)  Formal, centrally coordinated rotation programmes that provide structured opportunities to gain experience across agencies. If the page is not available, it should show the “Page not Found” error state.
