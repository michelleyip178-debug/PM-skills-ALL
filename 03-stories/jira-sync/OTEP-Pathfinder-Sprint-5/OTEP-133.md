# OTEP-133: Access the hub via a deep link from an EDM

**Type:** Story
**Status:** Backlog
**Assignee:** N/A
**Story Points:** N/A

---

## Description

User Story As an officer, I want to be able to click a link in an email and land directly on the relevant opportunity so that I can act on opportunities shared with me without navigating from scratch.   Acceptance Criteria If an officer lands on an opportunity they’re not eligible: they will see a clear message that they are not authorised to view this opportunity and surface alternative opportunities on the page. The look and feel of the page should be aligned with OTEP theme.
