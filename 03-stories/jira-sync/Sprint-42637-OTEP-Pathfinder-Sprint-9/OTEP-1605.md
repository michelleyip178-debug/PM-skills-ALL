## Description

Relying on the digest seems not the right approach (or our implementation is flawed) and we need to be mindful because it’sdifficult to catch outside testing in production mode (or in dev).    see  unauthorized.tsx  for such example where we had to make the component a  client  component to avoid scrambling.
