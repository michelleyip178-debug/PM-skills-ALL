# Research and Discovery Prompts

Prompts for synthesising research, identifying patterns, and mapping user needs.

---

## Synthesising interviews

> What are the top 5 findings across all interview notes? For each: the finding, how many participants mentioned it, a supporting quote, and a recommended action. Flag any contradictions between participants.

---

## Identifying patterns

> What themes or patterns appear across multiple sources? Group findings by theme. For each theme: frequency, strength of evidence, and whether it's actionable now or needs more research.

---

## Competitive context

> What do the sources say about competitors or alternative solutions? How does our approach differ? Where are we stronger? Where are we at risk?

---

## User needs mapping

> Map every user need or pain point mentioned across all sources. For each: who has it, how severe it is, which feature addresses it, and whether that feature is in MVP or R1.

---

## Jobs-to-be-done extraction

> What jobs are users trying to get done based on the research? For each job: the trigger situation, the desired outcome, the current workaround, and how frustrated they are with the workaround. Group by user type.

---

## Unmet needs finder

> Which user needs or pain points mentioned in research are NOT addressed by any current feature or story? List each gap. For the most critical ones, suggest a story or spike to close the gap.

---

## Evidence strength assessment

> For each major finding or assumption driving our product decisions: how strong is the evidence? Rate as: Strong (multiple sources, direct quotes), Moderate (mentioned in 2+ places but indirect), or Weak (single source or inferred). Flag any product decisions resting on weak evidence.

---

## Research gaps

> Based on what we know and what we've built, what do we still not know? List the top 5 unanswered questions that would change our approach if answered differently. For each: the question, what we'd do if the answer is A vs B, and a suggested research method to answer it.
