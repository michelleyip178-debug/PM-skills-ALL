# Sprint and Delivery Prompts

Prompts for querying your NotebookLM notebook during sprint ceremonies and delivery work.

---

## Before grooming

> For each user story in the notebook, rate readiness as Ready / Needs work / Blocked. Check: clear persona, defined outcome, acceptance criteria, no unresolved dependencies. For anything not Ready, say what's missing in one line.

---

## Before sprint planning

> What stories are ready to commit to next sprint? List each with: story ID, estimated size, dependencies, and any open questions that could block it.

---

## Mid-sprint check

> Based on the latest meeting notes and specs, what's on track, what's at risk, and what's blocked? Flag any scope creep — requirements that weren't in the original sprint commitment.

---

## Sprint review prep

> List everything that shipped or is demo-able this sprint. For each: what it does, who it's for, and the acceptance criteria it met. Order them for a stakeholder demo.

---

## After sprint

> What decisions were made this sprint? For each: the decision, who made it, the rationale, and whether it's documented in a spec or only in meeting notes.

---

## Story dependency map

> Map all dependencies between stories. For each: Story A depends on Story B for [reason]. Flag any circular dependencies or stories that block 3+ others.

---

## Velocity and capacity check

> Based on past sprint notes, how many stories did we complete per sprint? What's our realistic capacity for next sprint? Flag any upcoming holidays, leaves, or reduced availability mentioned in the sources.

---

## AC quality review

> Review all acceptance criteria across committed stories. Flag any that are: not testable, missing edge cases (empty states, errors, permissions), duplicated across stories, or contradicting another story's AC.
