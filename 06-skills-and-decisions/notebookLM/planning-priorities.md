# Planning and Priorities Prompts

Prompts for weekly planning, retros, and prioritisation.

---

## Monday planning

> Based on the latest meeting notes and documents: what decisions need to be made this week? What stakeholder conversations are urgent? What spec gaps need closing before next grooming? What's the single most important thing to move forward?

---

## Friday retro

> What happened this week based on meeting notes and updated docs? Use Signal-Sense-Shift: what signals are worth noting, what do they suggest, what should I do differently next week? Be specific and cite evidence.

---

## Prioritisation

> List all open work items, stories, and tasks mentioned across sources. Rank by: blocks other work > has a deadline > reduces risk > everything else. What should I do first on Monday?

---

## Risk-based prioritisation

> What are the top 5 risks to shipping on time based on all sources? For each: the risk, the likelihood, the impact if it hits, and what I can do this week to reduce it. Rank by expected impact.

---

## Stakeholder priority alignment

> What does each stakeholder care most about right now based on meeting notes and documents? Where do their priorities conflict? Where am I spending time that doesn't align with what matters most to the people who decide if this ships?

---

## Sprint goal options

> Based on what's ready, what's blocked, and what's most valuable: propose 2-3 sprint goal options for next sprint. For each: the goal in one sentence, which stories it includes, what it unblocks, and the main risk. Recommend one.

---

## Quarterly check-in

> How are we tracking against the quarterly OKRs mentioned in the sources? For each key result: current status, what's been delivered toward it, what's at risk, and what needs to happen in the remaining weeks. Be honest — flag anything off track.

---

## End-of-day triage

> Based on everything in the notebook: what's the one thing that moved forward today, the one thing that's stuck, and the one thing I should tackle first tomorrow morning? Keep it to three sentences.
