# 5. Understand what I'd actually be doing

**Persona:** Persona 1 - Officer
**Original Source Label:** 5. Understand what I'd actually be doing   [MVP]

## Job to be Done
- **When** I'm considering an opportunity
- **I want to** see enough detail to know what I'd do day-to-day, who I'd report to, what's expected, and how this fits my life
- **So I can** decide whether to invest effort in applying.
