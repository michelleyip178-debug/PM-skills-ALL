# Project Brief: BA-to-PM Conversion

**Owner:** Michelle Yip
**Target:** Formal PM title conversion via panel review
**Timeline:** Evidence gathering now through end of Q2 2026 (or whenever panel convenes)
**Status:** In Progress

---

## The Goal

Get the formal PM role conversion approved by a review panel. The title is a formality at the point where the evidence is undeniable — the job is to make the evidence visible and structured.

## What the Panel Needs to See

A committee reviews the case. They need evidence mapped to PM competencies — not a list of tasks completed, but demonstrated thinking and behaviour that distinguishes PM work from BA work.

### Three Competency Areas (mapped to my skill gaps)

| Competency | BA version | PM version | How I demonstrate it |
|------------|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Outcomes thinking** | Documenting requirements | Framing work around user/business outcomes before specs | Sprint goals as outcomes, PRD hypothesis, scope recommendations anchored to "what does success look like" |
| **Stakeholder influence** | Presenting options, letting others decide | Building a narrative, bringing people along, making recommendations | Categorisation research presented as a recommendation (not options), stakeholder briefs, scope decisions defended with rationale |
| **Roadmapping and prioritisation** | Listing everything, equal weight | Sequencing against outcomes, saying no, making trade-off calls | MVP vs R1 decisions with rationale, sprint plan sequencing, scoping gaps tracker with formal deferrals |

## What Success Looks Like

- Panel approves the conversion
- Adrian and Jace can point to specific artifacts that demonstrate PM-mode operating
- The evidence is produced naturally through OTEP delivery — not manufactured for the panel

## Two Deliverables

1. **Evidence tracker** (`evidence-tracker.md`) — living document, updated at every sprint boundary. Running list of PM-mode artifacts and moments, organised by competency area.

2. **One-pager** (`one-pager.md`) — polished narrative document for the panel. Updated monthly or before key conversations. Draws from the evidence tracker.

## Update Cadence

| Document | When to update | Trigger |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| Evidence tracker | Every sprint boundary (biweekly) | Sprint Boundary workflow, Step 2 |
| One-pager | Monthly, or before panel/Adrian 1:1 | Manual — review tracker, refresh narrative |
