# GOALS.md

## Who I Am

**Michelle Yip** | Business Analyst → Product Manager | GovTech Singapore

I'm in active transition from BA to PM. The shift I'm making: from translating requirements to driving outcomes; from presenting options to making recommendations; from owning the spec to owning the problem.

**How I want to operate:**
- Lead with outcomes, not requirements
- Recommend, don't just present options
- Build stakeholder influence and a sharable vision
- Hold the line on scope when MVP discipline is at stake

**Working style:**
- I decide faster from structure — tables, prompt cards, phased frameworks beat long prose
- I want feedback direct — name the problem, skip the cushioning; tell me when I've slipped into BA-mode
- I learn by analogy — a concrete comparison makes a new concept land

**What energizes me:**
- Making the call — deciding and owning the recommendation, not just laying out options
- Shaping a vision others rally around — the why, told well enough to bring people along
- Untangling a mess — turning an ambiguous problem into a clean structure or sequence
- Seeing things ship — draft → groomed → built → demo'd; visible movement, not just motion

**What drains me:**
- Documenting without a decision attached to it — BA-mode busywork
- Senior-stakeholder direction that's frequent but keeps shifting
- Back-to-back meetings with no protected block for deep work

## Personal Development Goals (2026)

The three gaps I'm closing in the BA→PM transition:

1. **Outcomes thinking** — frame work in terms of the user/business outcome before the spec; resist the pull to optimize the requirement.

2. **Stakeholder influence & vision** — build a sharable narrative for what we're doing and why; learn to bring stakeholders along rather than inform them after the fact.

3. **Roadmapping & prioritization** — develop the muscle to sequence work against outcomes and tradeoffs, not just deliverables.

**Anchor:** Reforge Product Foundations (Phase 1), supplemented by mentor + PM community touchpoints.
