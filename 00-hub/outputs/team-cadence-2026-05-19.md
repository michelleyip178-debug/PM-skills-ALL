## Sprint Pattern — Every 2 Weeks

### Week 1

| Day | Ceremony | Who |
|-----|----------|-----|
| Mon | Sprint starts · Demo + Retro (previous sprint) | Full team |
| Tue | Squad Grooming — next sprint (internal, 30 min) | Dev + PM |
| Thu | Backlog Grooming — next sprint (with Pow Hwee) | Full team |
| Fri | OTEP Squad Sync | Full team |

### Week 2

| Day | Ceremony | Who |
|-----|----------|-----|
| Mon | Mid-Sprint Review | Full team |
| Thu | Sprint Planning — next sprint | Full team |
| Thu | Sprint Review + Retro (same day, back-to-back) | Full team |
| Fri | Sprint ends |  |

> **Retro cadence:** Every sprint for S01–S03. From S04, every second sprint (S05, S07, S09, S11). Non-retro sprints: async check-in instead.
