# OTEP Squad Cadence Framework

> **For:** Pow Hwee
> **From:** Michelle
> **Date:** 19 May 2026
> **Purpose:** How often we should run our squad ceremonies across a 2-week sprint cycle

---

## TL;DR

We're on **2-week sprints**. Most ceremonies are **biweekly** (once per sprint), anchored to the same days each cycle. Daily standups are the only daily touchpoint.

---

## 🔁 Daily

| Ceremony | Time | Purpose |
|----------|------|---------|
| Team Standup | 11am, every day | Surface blockers and priority changes quickly |

---

## 📅 Week 1 of Each Sprint

| Day | Ceremony | Cadence | Purpose |
|-----|----------|---------|---------|
| **Monday** | Sprint Start + Retro & Demo *(prev sprint)* | Biweekly | Review what shipped; what to improve |
| **Tuesday** | Internal Squad Grooming | Biweekly | Draft and refine stories for the *next* sprint |
| **Thursday** | Backlog Grooming | Biweekly | Prioritised, DoR-ready stories confirmed |
| **Friday** | OTEP Squad Sync | Biweekly | Clear unresolved DoR blockers before mid-sprint |

---

## 📅 Week 2 of Each Sprint

| Day | Ceremony | Cadence | Purpose |
|-----|----------|---------|---------|
| **Monday** | Mid-Sprint Review | Biweekly | Flag at-risk items and scope changes early |
| **Thursday** | Sprint Planning | Biweekly | Lock in sprint goal + DoR-ready stories |
| **Friday** | Sprint End + Finalisation | Biweekly | Archive sprint; prep retro |
