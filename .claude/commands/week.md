# /week — Week-Ahead Planning

Read these files before generating output:
- `context/current-sprint.md` — sprint goal, dates, committed stories
- `context/open-items.md` — unresolved items with owners and deadlines
- `context/decisions-log.md` — recent scope and design decisions
- `context/risks.md` — active blockers and dependencies
- `projects/otep-mvp/sprint-checklists.md` — grooming readiness and DoR blockers
- `projects/sprint-allocation.md` — what's in the current and next sprint
- `tasks/active.md` — in-progress, up-next, and waiting-on items
- `GOALS.md` — priorities and success metrics

Help me plan the week ahead:

1. **Decisions needed this week** — Not next sprint, this week. What needs a call made? Cross-reference open-items.md and decisions-log.md for anything with a deadline this week.

2. **Stakeholder conversations** — What's urgent or overdue? Who do I need to align with before anything gets blocked? Check open-items.md owners and waiting-on items in active.md.

3. **Spec and story gaps to close** — What's missing before the next grooming or sprint? What will Pow Hwee ask about that I don't have an answer for yet? Check sprint-checklists.md DoR blockers.

4. **The single most important thing** — If I only move one thing forward this week, what should it be and why?

Be direct. Priorities, not a list of everything. PM mode, not BA mode.
