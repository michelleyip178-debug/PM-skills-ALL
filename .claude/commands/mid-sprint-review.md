# Mid-Sprint Review Prep

Prepare Michelle to participate in the mid-sprint review as a PM.
Her role here is to listen, surface risks, and decide if anything
needs to change — not to run the session.

Read CLAUDE.md, context/current-sprint.md, and context/open-items.md
before generating output.

---

## Steps

### Step 1 — Sprint health check
Based on context/current-sprint.md, assess:
- How many stories are committed vs completed so far?
- Are we on track to hit the sprint goal?
- Are any stories blocked or at risk?

Summarise as: 🟢 On track | 🟡 At risk | 🔴 Off track

### Step 2 — Identify blockers to raise
From context/open-items.md, surface any items that:
- Were assigned an owner but have not been resolved
- Are now blocking a story from being completed this sprint
- Have passed their needed-by date

### Step 3 — Scope creep check
Flag any work that may have been added to the sprint informally
(conversations in Slack, requests from stakeholders) that wasn't
part of the original sprint commitment.

### Step 4 — PM decisions needed
List any decisions that need Michelle to act before the sprint ends:
- AC clarifications Pow Hwee or the team is waiting on
- Design sign-offs needed from Amber
- Stakeholder confirmations from Jacky or Xian Zhang

### Step 5 — Generate briefing
