# R1 Strategic Rationale — Reference Note

**Created:** 2026-05-20
**Source:** CareerCompass OKR Review & Roadmap deck + PM synthesis
**Owner:** Michelle

---

## The Rationale

> "The key objective of R1 is to convert discovery into action — moving officers from finding an opportunity to completing an application, so that the platform demonstrates tangible career impact, not just engagement."

MVP solves the visibility problem — officers can find opportunities in one place. But visibility without a frictionless apply flow won't move the North Star. An officer who discovers an opportunity but drops off at a redirect, a blank form, or a status black hole hasn't taken a development action. R1 removes exactly those three failure points:

| Failure point | R1 fix |
|---|---|
| The redirect | In-app application (no external handoff) |
| The blank form | Pre-filled applications from officer profile |
| The status black hole | End-to-end tracking via ATS integration |

## Why the deprioritised items were cut

Smart Assistant, Gap Radar, and Intelligence Dashboard improve the *quality* of applications and *insight* for officers — but they don't remove friction from the critical path between "I want to apply" and "application submitted." They're R2 territory, where the platform shifts from enabling action to refining it.

The tighter scope also protects the 3-month delivery window (Jan–Mar '27). ATS integration is already a significant technical dependency; adding AI-driven features would stretch the team past what's achievable.

## North Star alignment

| Milestone | Target | Why this scope gets there |
|---|---|---|
| MVP (Oct '26) | Baseline | Officers discover opportunities |
| R1 (Mar '27) | 10% complete a development action | Officers can act on what they find — apply, save, track |
| R4 (Dec '27) | 30% | Advanced features + AI matching live |
| R8 (Dec '28) | 50% | Full AI-powered maturity |

## Strategic anchor (from Steering, 2026-03-12)

FormSG was always the MVP vehicle, not the long-term solution. Steering approved replacing FormSG with an OTEP-hosted application flow. R1 is where that direction becomes real — officers apply within CareerCompass, not via redirect.
