# 5. Co-author a development plan

**Persona:** Persona 4 - Supervisor
**Original Source Label:** 5. Co-author a development plan   [R3: Co-created Development Plans]

## Job to be Done
- **When** my report and I have agreed on direction
- **I want to** co-create a development plan we can both refer back to
- **So I can** ensure commitment is mutual and progress is visible.
