# 4. Help them think through fit, not just enthusiasm

**Persona:** Persona 4 - Supervisor
**Original Source Label:** 4. Help them think through fit, not just enthusiasm   [R3: Precision Gap Mapping; R4: Conversation Toolkit]

## Job to be Done
- **When** my report is excited about an opportunity that may not be right for them
- **I want to** have a structured way to discuss fit — competencies, timing, career arc
- **So I can** challenge constructively without crushing initiative.
