## Decisions

### When to include BOs

| # | Stage | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Pre-backlog | Include BOs when framing the problem and scope for a new feature/epic |
| D2 | Pre-backlog | Include BOs when problem statement, user segment, and risk trade-offs need clarification |
| D3 | Pre-backlog | Include BOs when deciding what is in/out of the backlog for an initiative |
| D4 | Sprint lock | Confirm with BOs the candidate stories for Sprint 2/3 and their relative priority |
| D5 | Sprint lock | Align on must-haves vs nice-to-haves across modules (e.g. competencies, opportunities, job posting) |
| D6 | Sprint lock | Align on dependencies that might block meaningful progress |
| D7 | Ad hoc | Pull BOs into grooming only when a story is blocked on a business rule/policy or needs a business decision |

### When to keep BOs out

| # | Stage | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D8 | Grooming | Keep BOs out of full grooming/sizing sessions by default |
| D9 | Engineering | Keep BOs out of engineering-heavy discussions unless a specific decision is needed |

## Rules of thumb (summary)

**Include BOs when:**
- A new problem/epic is being framed
- Priority or scope decisions affect multiple squads/modules
- Presenting sprint goals for alignment
- A story is blocked on a business decision or policy

**Keep BOs out of:**
- Routine detailed grooming/sizing
- Engineering-heavy discussions (unless a specific decision is needed)
